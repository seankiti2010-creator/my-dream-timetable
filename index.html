About My Dream School

My dream school is a place where students learn, develop their talents, make friends and prepare for a successful future. The school has qualified teachers, modern classrooms, a library, a computer laboratory and a sports field.

My Morning Routine

These are the things I do before going to school:

  1. Wake up early in the morning.
  2. Brush my teeth and take a bath.
  3. Pray and have breakfast.
  4. Put on my school uniform.
  5. Pack my books and school materials.
  6. Go to school on time.

Features of My Dream School
